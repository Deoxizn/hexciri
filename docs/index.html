Highlights

The whole pitch, six cards.

A menu that does things

9 root entries, ~60 leaves — packages, sharing, hardware, themes, network, security, maintenance, gaming. Every row works. Root is Mod+Alt+Space; Esc always goes back.

Themes that color everything

hexciri-theme set recolors terminals, editors, browsers, Discord, GTK/Qt, Spotify, the bar, lock screen and login in one hook. 22 defaults ship; extras are one line each.

Niri or Hyprland

Same menu, themes and keys on both. hexciri-wm-switch moves you over with configs converted and defaults flipped — no reinstall.

Apps in, cruft out — once

Install adds kitty, Zed, OpenCode, Brave Origin and friends, and removes what they replace. Afterwards your choices stick.

Wallpapers you keep

Drop images in a folder; they join every theme's picker and survive every swap.

Search, calc, clipboard

Mod+Shift+S searches the web, Mod+Shift+C calculates, Mod+Ctrl+V recalls everything you copied. Menu modes, not apps.

The keys that matter

Mod = Super. Search them all live with Mod+K.
